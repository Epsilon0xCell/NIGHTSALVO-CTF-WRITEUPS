# Tier Ladder

Tiers are counted on published, genuinely solved writeups only. A writeup counts when it is in this repo with its captured flag.

| Tier | Requirement |
|---|---|
| INITIATE | 10 writeups across 3 categories |
| OPERATOR | 25 writeups across 5 categories and 3 events |
| BREAKER | 50 writeups across all 6 categories and 5 events |
| SALVO | 100 writeups and 10 events, plus a first live CTFtime event played under the NightSalvo name |
| MYTH | 250 writeups, 20 events, a top 25 percent finish in a rated live event, and writeups referenced by other people |
| LEGEND | 500 writeups, 40 events, a top 10 percent finish in a major rated event, and community citations of the archive |

Beyond the ladder: CANON is when other players learn from the archive before their own events; it is conferred, never claimed.

## Tier notification rule

Every tier change is announced to the owner when it lands. The announcement names the old tier, the new tier, and the metric that crossed.
